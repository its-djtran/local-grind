A row of houses lines a street, and `nums[i]` is the amount of cash stored in house `i`. You plan to rob some of them, but neighboring houses share an alarm system: if **two adjacent houses** are both robbed on the same night, the police are called.

Return the **largest total amount** you can collect without ever robbing two neighboring houses.

<!-- examples -->

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= nums.length <= 100`
- `0 <= nums[i] <= 400`
