An elevation map is described by an array `height` of `n` non-negative integers: column `i` is a bar of width `1` and height `height[i]`, and the bars stand side by side.

After a heavy rain, water settles in the dips between taller bars, and any water that is not held in on both sides runs off the ends of the map. Return the total number of unit squares of water that the map holds.

<!-- examples -->

**Constraints:**

- `n == height.length`
- `1 <= n <= 2 * 10^4`
- `0 <= height[i] <= 10^5`
