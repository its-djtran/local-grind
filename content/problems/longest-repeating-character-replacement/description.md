You are given a string `s` of uppercase English letters and an integer `k`. You may perform at most `k` operations, where one operation picks any position in `s` and replaces its letter with any other uppercase letter.

Return the length of the longest substring that consists of a **single repeated letter** after performing at most `k` operations.

<!-- examples -->

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= s.length <= 10^5`
- `s` contains only uppercase English letters.
- `0 <= k <= s.length`
