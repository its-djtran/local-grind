You are given two strings, `text1` and `text2`. Return the length of the **longest common subsequence** of the two, or `0` if they have nothing in common.

A **subsequence** of a string is what remains after deleting any number of its characters (possibly none) while keeping the remaining characters in their original order. For instance, `"bd"` is a subsequence of `"abcd"`, while `"db"` is not.

A **common subsequence** is a string that is a subsequence of both `text1` and `text2`.

<!-- examples -->

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= text1.length, text2.length <= 1000`
- Both strings consist only of lowercase English letters.
