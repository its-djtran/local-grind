You are given an array `prices` in which `prices[i]` is the price of a certain stock on day `i`.

You may make at most one trade: pick one day to **buy** a single share and a **later** day to **sell** it. Return the largest profit you can earn this way. If no trade makes money, return `0`.

<!-- examples -->

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= prices.length <= 10^5`
- `0 <= prices[i] <= 10^4`
